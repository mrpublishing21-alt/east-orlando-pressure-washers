Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about pool deck cleaning in Orlando

How much does pool deck cleaning cost?

A smaller basic pool deck typically runs $100–$200. Larger decks with heavy algae, calcium buildup, or travertine/cool-deck surfaces can range from $300–$400+. We also offer pool cage/screen enclosure cleaning for $125–$350 (higher for two-story cages, dense algae, or fragile screens). Bundled pool deck + cage + lanai packages run $300–$575. Call (407) 499-4773 for a personalized quote.

Why use soft wash instead of pressure washing?

Pool decks often have delicate surfaces that can be damaged by high-pressure water. Soft washing uses specialized cleaning solutions at low pressure to safely remove algae without damaging pavers or concrete.
