Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about fence cleaning in Orlando

How much does fence cleaning cost?

Vinyl fencing runs $1.00–$3.00 per linear foot (typical 100 ft: $100–$300). Charge toward the top for 6–8 ft height, both sides, tight landscaping, oxidation, or heavy algae. Wooden fencing is $1.50–$3.50 per linear foot ($150–$350 for 100 ft) — we use soft-wash techniques to protect old wood and peeling paint/stain. Call (407) 499-4773 for a personalized quote.

Can you clean both vinyl and wood fences?

Yes! We use different techniques for each material to safely remove algae from vinyl and weathering from wood without damaging either surface or surrounding landscaping.
