Restore your home's curb appeal with professional driveway cleaning. We remove years of algae and mold stains, tire marks, and slippery grime from your concrete and pavers.

Why Regular Driveway Cleaning Matters

Safety First

Algae and moss make driveways dangerously slippery — especially after Florida's frequent rain showers. Regular cleaning prevents slips and falls for your family and visitors.

Protect Your Investment

Florida's humidity and UV exposure accelerate concrete degradation. Removing organic growth and stains extends the life of your driveway and prevents costly repairs.

Boost Curb Appeal

A clean driveway dramatically improves your home's appearance and can increase property value. First impressions matter — make yours count.

Frequently Asked Questions About Driveway Cleaning

Common questions about our driveway pressure washing services in Orlando, FL

How much does driveway cleaning cost in Orlando?

A small driveway (under 400 sq. ft.) typically runs $100–$150. A typical 400–600 sq. ft. concrete driveway is about $150–$200. Larger driveways (800+ sq. ft.), pavers, or jobs with dense algae and oil stains range from $200–$325+. We offer free estimates so you know exactly what to expect — call (407) 499-4773 for a personalized quote.

How often should I pressure wash my driveway?

We recommend driveway cleaning at least once per year in the Orlando area. Florida's humidity, rain, and sun accelerate algae, mold, and mildew buildup on concrete surfaces. If you have heavy traffic or nearby trees dropping debris, twice a year may be beneficial.

Can you remove oil stains from my driveway?

We can significantly reduce or eliminate most oil stains through our professional cleaning process. Heavy oil buildup may require multiple treatments for best results. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

Do you clean both concrete and paver driveways?

Yes! We safely clean both concrete and paver driveways using the appropriate technique for each surface type. Pavers may require slightly different pressure settings to avoid damaging the joints.
