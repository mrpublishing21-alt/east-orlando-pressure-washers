Common questions about patio cleaning in Orlando

How much does patio or lanai cleaning cost?

A small slab patio runs $75–$125; a normal patio is typically $125–$200. Large paver/concrete patios with heavy growth can reach $200–$300+. Open or covered/screened lanais run $100–$275 depending on floor, walls, and ceiling coverage. If the quote includes pool cage screens/frame, we combine it as a pool-enclosure package. Call (407) 499-4773 for a personalized quote.

Can you restore discolored pavers?

Yes! Our cleaning process removes years of algae, mold and weather-related discoloration from pavers, restoring them to their original color and appearance.
