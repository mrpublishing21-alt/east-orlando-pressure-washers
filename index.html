Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our pressure washing services in Orlando, FL

How much does pressure washing cost in Orlando?

The cost of pressure washing in Orlando varies by surface size, condition, and provider — typically $0.10–$0.75 per square foot overall. Here are typical ranges for standard residential jobs: Driveways (400–1,200 sq ft) run $100–$400. Pool decks start at $300–$600 for basic cleaning. Fences (100 linear ft) cost $150–$500. Patios (100–400 sq ft) range from $78–$500. House washing for 1,500–3,000 sq ft homes costs $200–$900. We offer free estimates so you know exactly what to expect — call (407) 499-4773 for a personalized quote.

How often should I pressure wash my driveway?

We recommend pressure washing your driveway at least once per year in the Orlando area. Florida's humidity, rain, and sun accelerate the buildup of algae, mold, and mildew on concrete surfaces. If you have heavy traffic or nearby trees dropping debris, twice a year may be beneficial. Regular maintenance not only keeps your property looking great but also prevents costly damage over time.

What is soft washing vs pressure washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (2,500-4,000 PSI) to blast away dirt, grime, and mold from durable surfaces like concrete driveways, pool decks, and brick. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized cleaning solutions to gently clean delicate surfaces like house siding, roofs, and vinyl fencing without causing damage. We use the right method for each surface to get the best results safely.
