Service & Methods

What is soft washing vs pressure washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (2,500-4,000 PSI) to blast away dirt, grime, and mold from durable surfaces like concrete driveways, pool decks, and brick. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized cleaning solutions to gently clean delicate surfaces like house siding, roofs, and vinyl fencing without causing damage. We use the right method for each surface to get the best results safely.

Is pressure washing safe for my property?

Yes, when done by professionals. We use the appropriate technique for each surface — high-pressure for durable surfaces like concrete and low-pressure soft wash for delicate surfaces like stucco and vinyl siding. Our licensed and insured team ensures your property is protected.

How long does pressure washing take?

Most residential jobs are completed in 1–3 hours depending on the size of the area and level of soiling. We'll provide an estimated timeframe during your free consultation.

Maintenance & Care

How often should I pressure wash my driveway?

We recommend pressure washing your driveway at least once per year in the Orlando area. Florida's humidity, rain, and sun accelerate the buildup of algae, mold, and mildew on concrete surfaces. If you have heavy traffic or nearby trees dropping debris, twice a year may be beneficial.
